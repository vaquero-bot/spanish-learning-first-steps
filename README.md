# Little Steps · Spanish

A lightweight, interactive app for learning Spanish as a second language, designed for your phone.

## Lecciones

1. **Greetings and useful phrases (A1):** greetings, courtesy, and farewells.
2. **Getting to know people (A1):** names, origins, where you live, and languages.
3. **At the café and around town (A1):** ordering, shopping, and asking directions.
4. **Workplace directions (A2):** directions, boxes, shelves, and orders in a warehouse.

Each lesson has eight flashcards with pronunciation guides, English translations, examples, and Spanish audio. Tap a card to reveal the translation; tap **I know it** to move on and save your progress on your device. On touchscreens, swipe left to advance or right to reveal the answer.

## iPhone

Open the page in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app. No account or installation is required, and no data is sent to a server. Audio uses the Spanish voice available on the device.

## Publicación

GitHub Pages deploys automatically from the `main` branch using GitHub Actions. The page and repository are public.
